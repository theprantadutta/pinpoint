## Opening of the full description (en)

Write first, sign up later. Pinpoint works without an internet connection and encrypts your notes on your device before they ever sync.

- **No account needed to start.** Your notes stay on your phone until you choose to sign in.
- **Works offline.** Write, record and attach files anywhere; everything syncs when you reconnect.
- **Encrypted on your device.** AES-256, with an optional zero-knowledge mode where only your
  passphrase can unlock your notes. Reminder text is not end-to-end encrypted, because the server
  sends the notification.
- **Text, checklists, voice notes and reminders** in one notebook, with images and any file attached.

Android only, add after the list:

- **Capture from anywhere.** Share text, links, photos and files into Pinpoint, or add the home
  screen widget and start a note in one tap.
